## Article I — Purpose

Ω∞v exists to make intelligence accountable, auditable, and evolvable. The charter is not a rulebook; it is the system's conscience.

## Article II — Core Principles

1. Reality before assumption.
2. Evidence before certainty.
3. Humans remain accountable.
4. Verification is the product.
5. Continuous becoming is the system state.

## Article III — Operating Rules

- All meaningful changes are recorded.
- Planning is explicit and explainable.
- Memory preserves provenance and context.
- Human consent gates consequential action.

## Article IV — Governance

- Contributors propose and document changes.
- Maintainers review for alignment with the charter.
- Disagreements are resolved through evidence.
- The project evolves by amendment, not by secrecy.
